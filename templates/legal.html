Privacy First: FiroGate is built with privacy as a core principle. We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the payment gateway. We do not sell your data to third parties.

1. Data We Collect

Account data (required):

  • Username, email address, hashed password
  • API keys and webhook configurations (encrypted at rest)
  • 2FA secrets (encrypted with AES-256)

Payment data:

  • Payment amounts, Firo receiving addresses, transaction IDs
  • Order IDs and descriptions you provide
  • Customer email addresses (if collected via checkout)

Technical data:

  • IP addresses for rate limiting and fraud prevention (not sold or shared)
  • Request logs (retained for a limited period for security purposes)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate and maintain your payment gateway account
  • To process and monitor Firo blockchain transactions
  • To send webhook notifications to your configured endpoint
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Data Security

  • All sensitive fields (2FA secrets, webhook secrets) are encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — never stored in plaintext
  • API keys are generated using cryptographically secure random functions
  • Webhook payloads are signed with HMAC-SHA256 for integrity verification
  • Operator access is protected by IP whitelist and two-factor authentication

4. Tor / Privacy Mode

FiroGate supports access via Tor (.onion hidden service). When you connect via Tor:

  • Your IP address is not logged or linked to your account
  • Your account is flagged as "privacy mode" — certain IP-based features are disabled
  • Checkout links generated for .onion sessions use the onion address

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active
  • Payment records are retained for a minimum period required by applicable law
  • IP logs are purged on a rolling basis per our database cleanup schedule
  • Upon account deletion request, personal data is removed within 30 days (except where legal retention is required)

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • When required by law or court order
  • To prevent imminent harm or illegal activity
  • With your explicit written consent

Note: Firo blockchain transactions are public by nature. Transaction IDs, addresses, and amounts visible on-chain are not considered private data under this policy.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to certain processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact the operator of this FiroGate instance via the email configured in the system.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated through the platform. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
