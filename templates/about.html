About FiroGate

FiroGate is a self-hosted, non-custodial payment gateway for Firo. It runs on your own server, connects to your own Firo node, and every payment settles directly to your wallet.

How it works

Connect a Spark view key, create an invoice through the dashboard or the API, and FiroGate watches the blockchain for the matching payment. Once confirmed, a signed webhook notifies your backend automatically.

No custody

FiroGate never holds a spend key. Addresses are derived from your view key and funds move directly from customer to merchant on-chain.

How payments are confirmed

FiroGate decides a payment is final using one thing only: how many blocks have been mined on top of it your Confirmation Policy (Instant / Fast / Standard / High Security), which you set for your instance. A feature called blocknotify just wakes the scanner the moment a new block arrives instead of waiting on a timer, so detection is fast but it never changes what counts as confirmed. Firo's own network-level ChainLocks are what make trusting plain block confirmations safe, so FiroGate doesn't need to check them itself. You may also see an "InstantLock" flag on some Firo transactions FiroGate deliberately does not use it to confirm payments, because it isn't reliably available for every Spark payment and would be an unsafe thing to depend on for settlement.

Open source

FiroGate is a complete payment platform, fully open source. Self-host it, customize it, extend it however your infrastructure needs.
