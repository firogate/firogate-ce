Community Edition

A Firo payment gateway you run yourself.

Non-custodial. FiroGate runs on your server, talks to your own Firo node, and every payment goes straight to your wallet.

How it works

Four steps from setup to paid.

  1. Connect your walletAdd a Spark view key or an xpub from your own wallet.
  2. Create a paymentFrom the dashboard, a pay link or the API.
  3. The buyer paysFiroGate watches your node for the payment.
  4. You get paidThe money is already in your wallet, and your webhook fires.

Your keys stay yours

FiroGate only gets a view key and an xpub. They can see payments. They cannot move money.

There is no spend key on the server, so there is nothing on it that can send your funds.

When a payment counts

You choose in Settings.

Instant
0 confirmations

A new block wakes FiroGate right away. Only confirmations decide when a payment is paid.
