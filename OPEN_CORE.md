# Open-Core Model — FiroGate

FiroGate follows an **open-core model**.

The Community Edition (this repository) is fully functional for self-hosted deployments. The Enterprise Edition adds advanced infrastructure features for hosted/managed deployments.

---

## Enterprise Edition (Private)

The Enterprise Edition extends the Community Edition with additional infrastructure features for managed deployments.

Details are not publicly disclosed.

Contact: enterprise@firogate.com

---

## Why Open-Core?

- The Community Edition is complete and production-ready for self-hosted use
- Advanced infrastructure components require significant ongoing development
- Enterprise features involve operational risk and require dedicated support
